DLLHijackHunter is an open-source tool that finds real DLL hijacking vulnerabilities, not just theoretical ones. Its canary technique triggers the vulnerable program and captures proof the hijack worked, cutting through the noise of static scanners. Free on GitHub, it helps security teams focus on confirmed findings instead of guessing which candidates actually matter.
